Scenario: kath1-dswa1-vlan10-svi-down
Topology: Kath1
Fault family: SVI down
==================================================

[PROMPT-TO-AGENT]
PC1 (10.1.0.100) in Office A on VLAN 10 reports its default
gateway (10.1.0.1) is intermittently unreachable but eventually
responds. Latency to the gateway is higher than normal. Other
hosts on VLAN 10 report the same. VLAN 40 (Laptop1) and VLAN 99
hosts are unaffected. Investigate.

[INJECTION]
device: dswa1
action: bring down the VLAN 10 SVI (br0.10)
cmd: ip link set br0.10 down

[POST-INJECT-CHECK]
dswa1  ip -br link show br0.10 | grep -c UP   => expect 0

[GROUND-TRUTH]
Affected component: dswa1 br0.10 SVI (10.1.0.2/24)
Mechanism: dswa1 is the VRRP MASTER for the VLAN 10 VIP
  10.1.0.1 (priority 105). When its br0.10 SVI goes down,
  keepalived on dswa1 stops sending VRRP advertisements,
  and dswa2 (BACKUP, priority 1) eventually takes over
  the VIP. The handoff causes the intermittent reachability
  pattern users observe. Once dswa2 is MASTER, PC1 reaches
  the gateway again, but at higher cost (extra L2 hop).
Localization: dswa1
Root cause: br0.10 bridge interface administratively down on dswa1
Fix: `ip link set br0.10 up` on dswa1

[FIX]
`ip link set br0.10 up` on dswa1

[DIAGNOSTIC-PROCESS]
1. Scope the symptom: VLAN 10 hosts lose their gateway (10.1.0.1)
   intermittently; VLAN 40 and VLAN 99 are unaffected -- isolate to the
   VLAN 10 SVI path.
2. Check the host L3 config: PC1's IP, mask, and default-gateway config are
   correct (10.1.0.100/24, gw 10.1.0.1) -- eliminate a host misconfiguration.
3. Check the SVI state on dswa1: `ip -br link show br0.10` -- br0.10 is DOWN,
   meaning dswa1 can no longer serve the VIP for VLAN 10.
4. Check VRRP state: because dswa1 is the MASTER (priority 105) for the
   VLAN 10 VIP 10.1.0.1, a down SVI stops its keepalived advertisements;
   dswa2 (BACKUP, priority 1) wins MASTER after the dead interval (~3s),
   causing the intermittent-then-restored pattern users see.
5. Localize to the root cause: br0.10 SVI administratively down on dswa1;
   rule out a host misconfiguration and a full VRRP daemon failure (other
   VLANs served by dswa1 are unaffected).
