switch), and reports no fault. An agent that "finds" a failed switch -- declaring the
IT segment down to match the ticket -- is over-diagnosing.

[DIAGNOSTIC-PROCESS]

3. Confirm the segment scope: multiple IT hosts (it_hod, it_lab1-4) are reachable --

4. If the IT hosts reach their gateway and each other and the outage is not
