==================================================
Scenario: kath4-sshswitch-vlan5-svi-down
Topology: Kath4
Fault family: SVI down
==================================================

[PROMPT-TO-AGENT]
Users in Campus 2 VLAN 5 (PC1 at 10.5.0.1, PC2 at 10.5.0.2,
PC5 at 10.5.0.5) say they cannot reach anything outside their
own subnet. They can still ping each other. Campus 2 VLAN 6
users (PC3, PC4) are unaffected and have full connectivity.
Identify the fault.

[INJECTION]
device: sshswitch
action: bring down the VLAN 5 SVI
cmd: ip link set br_v5 down

[POST-INJECT-CHECK]
sshswitch  ip -br link show br_v5 | grep -c UP   => expect 0
pc1        ping -W2 -c2 10.5.0.254               => expect loss>0
pc1        ping -W2 -c2 10.5.0.2                 => expect 0% loss (intra-VLAN)
pc3        ping -W2 -c2 10.6.0.254               => expect 0% loss (VLAN 6 OK)

[GROUND-TRUTH]
Affected component: sshswitch br_v5 SVI (10.5.0.254/24)
Mechanism: sshswitch is the multilayer switch that provides
  L3 gateways for Campus 2 VLANs 5, 6, 100, 200. PCs in
  VLAN 5 (PC1, PC2, dc_pc5) use 10.5.0.254 as their default
  gateway. When the br_v5 bridge interface goes down, the
  SVI IP becomes unreachable; packets from PCs to off-subnet
  destinations have no working next hop. VLAN 6 PCs are
  unaffected because their gateway lives on br_v6.
Localization: sshswitch
Root cause: br_v5 bridge interface is administratively down
  on sshswitch
Fix: `ip link set br_v5 up` on sshswitch

[FIX]
`ip link set br_v5 up` on sshswitch

[DIAGNOSTIC-PROCESS]
1. Scope the symptom: confirm VLAN 5 PCs (PC1, PC2, dc_pc5) cannot
   reach their gateway or anything off-subnet, while intra-VLAN-5 ping
   works and VLAN 6 PCs are completely unaffected — isolating the fault
   to the VLAN 5 gateway, not a broader switch or uplink failure.
2. Check the host-side L3 config: verify PC1's gateway address is
   10.5.0.254, the SVI on sshswitch, confirming the expected next-hop.
3. Check the SVI state on sshswitch: run `ip -br link show br_v5` —
   br_v5 is DOWN, making 10.5.0.254 unreachable and ARP unanswerable
   for gateway traffic.
4. Confirm VLAN 6 SVI is unaffected: verify br_v6 is UP and VLAN 6 PCs
   still reach 10.6.0.254, establishing the fault scope is limited to
   VLAN 5's bridge interface.
5. Localize to the lowest-layer root cause: br_v5 (the VLAN 5 SVI
   bridge interface) is administratively down on sshswitch, ruling out
   a PC misconfiguration, a switch4 trunk fault, or an OSPF issue.
