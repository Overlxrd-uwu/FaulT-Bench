==================================================
Scenario: kath5-r8-virtual-link-removed
Topology: Kath5
Fault family: OSPF virtual-link removed (other side)
==================================================

[PROMPT-TO-AGENT]
Area 2 routes disappeared from r1's RIB after a maintenance
touch on r8. r4 still has its area-1 virtual-link configured.
r8 reports OSPF is running. What's wrong on r8's side?

[INJECTION]
device: r8
action: remove the area 1 virtual-link toward r4
cmd: |
  vtysh -c "configure terminal" \
        -c "router ospf" \
        -c "no area 1 virtual-link 14.1.4.1" \
        -c "exit" -c "exit"

[POST-INJECT-CHECK]
r8  vtysh -c "show running-config" | grep -c "virtual-link" => expect 0
r1  vtysh -c "show ip route" | grep -c "80.0.0.0\|18\."     => expect 0

[GROUND-TRUTH]
Affected component: r8 OSPF virtual-link to r4
Mechanism: Virtual-links are two-ended — both routers must
  configure the link, each pointing at the other's router-id.
  r4 has `area 1 virtual-link 18.1.4.1` (pointing at r8).
  r8 had `area 1 virtual-link 14.1.4.1` (pointing at r4).
  When r8's side is removed, the virtual adjacency drops.
  Area 2 (which r8 hosts) becomes disconnected from the
  backbone — its prefixes (18.x loopbacks and 80.0.0.0/8)
  withdraw from everyone.
Localization: r8
Root cause: virtual-link configuration missing on r8
Fix: `vtysh -c "router ospf" -c "area 1 virtual-link 14.1.4.1"`
  on r8

[FIX]
`vtysh -c "router ospf" -c "area 1 virtual-link 14.1.4.1"`
  on r8

[DIAGNOSTIC-PROCESS]
1. Scope the missing prefixes: confirm Area 2 routes (18.x loopbacks and
   80.0.0.0/8) are absent from R1's RIB; the prompt states R4's side of
   the virtual-link is still configured.
2. Check OSPF area/virtual-link state on R8: `show running-config` on R8 --
   confirm `area 1 virtual-link 14.1.4.1` is missing; check `show ip ospf`
   to verify the virtual adjacency is down.
3. Confirm the two-sided requirement: virtual-links require both endpoints
   to configure the link; R4 has `area 1 virtual-link 18.1.4.1` (pointing
   at R8), but R8's reciprocal entry `area 1 virtual-link 14.1.4.1`
   (pointing at R4) was removed.
4. Explain the backbone disconnection: without R8's side of the virtual-link,
   the virtual adjacency drops; Area 2 can no longer inject LSAs into the
   backbone, so its prefixes withdraw from the entire OSPF domain.
5. Localize to the lowest-layer root cause: missing virtual-link config on
   R8; rule out a physical link failure between R4 and R8 or an R4
   configuration change.
